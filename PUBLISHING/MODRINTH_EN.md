Acid Rain turns normal Minecraft weather into a clear survival threat. When a rain event becomes acidic, exposed Survival and Adventure players must find cover or rely on crafted chemical protection.

## What it adds

- A 25% chance for a newly detected rain event to become acid rain.
- Damage to exposed Survival and Adventure players during an acid storm.
- Green particles, sound cues, titles, action-bar status, and armor warnings.
- A four-piece chemical-protection set with vanilla crafting recipes.
- A separate corrosion system for chemical armor.
- Reduced corrosion risk when the armor has Unbreaking.
- Operator commands for status checks, testing, reset, and uninstall.

## How survival works

Acid rain only threatens players who are exposed to the sky. A roof or other solid cover provides shelter. Creative and Spectator players are ignored.

Wearing the complete chemical-protection set prevents the outdoor acid damage. Protection is not free: exposure gradually adds corrosion to the armor. The datapack reports that corrosion to the player and warns when a piece is close to failure. Unbreaking lowers the chance that a corrosion step is applied.

## Crafting

The release contains recipes for the Chemical Protection Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots. The crafting sheet in the project gallery shows the exact 1.21.11 layouts.

## Operator commands

- `/function x1miya:acid_rain/status` — show the current weather state and detected protection.
- `/function x1miya:acid_rain/force_start` — start a test acid-rain event.
- `/function x1miya:acid_rain/force_stop` — stop the forced event.
- `/function x1miya:acid_rain/reset` — reset the datapack state.
- `/function x1miya:acid_rain/uninstall` — remove datapack state before uninstalling.

## Installation

1. Download one language version.
2. Place the ZIP directly in `<world>/datapacks/`.
3. Enter the world or run `/reload`.
4. Use the status command if you want to confirm the current state.

Do not install the English and Russian ZIPs at the same time; they are alternative localizations of the same datapack.
